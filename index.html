Questions

Frequently asked

How fast can you get to me?

Typically 30–60 minutes, if that. Exact ETA depends on where you are and what is already on our board — call and we will tell you straight.

Are you really open 24/7?

Yes — nights, weekends and early mornings. We are often closed on major holidays, but we still answer for genuine emergencies, so call anyway.

How much does roadside assistance cost?

Roadside assistance — jump starts, lockouts, spare tire installs and fluid delivery — starts at $65. Mobile tire installation runs $225–$265 for passenger vehicles with the tire included, and $375+ for work and fleet trucks. Mobile battery replacement runs $189.99–$400.

Will you match another company’s quote?

If you have a lower quote, we will match it. Have the other company’s number ready when you call.

What payment methods do you take?

Credit and debit cards, PayPal, Venmo, Cash App, Apple Pay, Google Pay and cash. All sales are final.

Do you do mechanical repairs or rim repair?

No. We stick to towing and light-duty roadside work. If you need mechanical work or a rim repaired, ask us — we will refer you to a shop that handles it.

Can you open my car without damaging it?

Yes. Our lockout service is safe and damage free — it is in our name and it is what we do best.

Do you take my motor club or insurance roadside coverage?

We work with Agero, Allied Dispatch Solutions, Nation Safe Drivers, Quest Roadside, Allstate, AAA, Roadside Protect, Good Sam Roadside, Mach 1 Services, Honk Technologies and RapiTow.
